JUJUTSU KAISEN

Jujutsu Kaisen Season 2

Overview

Jujutsu Kaisen Season 2 (2023) is darker, more emotional, and more intense than Season 1. It was produced by MAPPA and adapts major arcs from the manga by Gege Akutami.

Structure

1. Hidden Inventory / Premature Death Arc (Episodes 1–5)

  • Set in 2006 during Gojo and Geto’s student days
  • They must protect Riko Amanai, the Star Plasma Vessel
  • Mission: safely deliver her to Master Tengen
  • Main enemy: Toji Fushiguro

Key Moments

  • Toji defeats Gojo
  • Gojo awakens his full power
  • Geto begins his descent into darkness

2. Shibuya Incident Arc (Episodes 6–23)

  • Set on October 31, 2018 in Shibuya
  • Main villains: Kenjaku, Mahito, Jogo, Hanami
  • Goal: Seal Gojo using the Prison Realm

Major Events

Gojo Gets Sealed

Gojo fights multiple special-grade curses but is sealed due to strategic planning by Kenjaku.

Sukuna’s Rampage

Sukuna takes control of Yuji Itadori and destroys large parts of Shibuya while fighting Jogo.

Major Deaths

  • Kento Nanami – killed by Mahito
  • Nobara Kugisaki – critically injured (uncertain fate)
  • Mechamaru – dies earlier in the arc

Yuji’s Breakdown

Yuji suffers severe emotional trauma after witnessing destruction and death caused by Sukuna.

Yuji & Todo vs Mahito

Aoi Todo teams up with Yuji in a major battle against Mahito.

Kenjaku’s Plan

Kenjaku absorbs Mahito and sets up the next arc: the Culling Game.

Animation & Music

  • Studio: MAPPA
  • Opening songs: "Ao no Sumika" (Tatsuya Kitani), "SPECIALZ" (King Gnu)
  • Ending song: "Akari" (Soushi Sakiyama)

Themes

  • The cost of power
  • Moral ambiguity
  • Trauma and loss
  • Collapse of safety (Gojo being sealed)

Why Season 2 Is Popular

  • Darker and more mature story
  • High emotional impact
  • Constant major plot twists
  • Top-tier fight animation

What It Sets Up

  • Power vacuum without Gojo
  • Yuji facing execution again
  • The upcoming Culling Game arc

Sturo Gojo

Satoru Gojo – Complete Breakdown (All Seasons)

Overview

Satoru Gojo is the strongest jujutsu sorcerer in Jujutsu Kaisen. Known for his overwhelming power, confidence, and unique abilities, he plays a central role across all seasons.

Personality

  • Confident and often playful
  • Extremely intelligent and strategic
  • Cares deeply about his students
  • Believes in changing the jujutsu world

Abilities & Powers

  • Limitless Technique: Allows control over space at an atomic level
  • Infinity: Creates an invisible barrier that slows attacks infinitely
  • Six Eyes: Enhances perception and cursed energy efficiency
  • Domain Expansion – Unlimited Void: Overwhelms enemies with infinite information
  • Reversed Cursed Technique: Self-healing ability

Season 1 Role

  • Introduced as Yuji Itadori’s mentor
  • Trains students at Tokyo Jujutsu High
  • Shows his strength in battles (e.g., vs Jogo)
  • Acts as a protective force against major threats

Jujutsu Kaisen 0 (Movie)

  • Guides Yuta Okkotsu
  • Fights against Suguru Geto
  • Reveals his past friendship with Geto

Season 2 – Hidden Inventory Arc

  • Shown as a student alongside Suguru Geto
  • Assigned to protect Riko Amanai
  • Defeated by Toji Fushiguro initially
  • Unlocks full power after near-death experience
  • Becomes “the strongest” after mastering his abilities

Season 2 – Shibuya Incident Arc

  • Fights multiple special-grade curses alone
  • Overwhelms enemies with his power
  • Gets sealed inside the Prison Realm by Kenjaku
  • His absence creates chaos in the jujutsu world

Fighting Style

  • Mix of hand-to-hand combat and cursed techniques
  • Uses speed, teleportation-like movement, and precision
  • Dominates opponents with minimal effort

Relationships

  • Yuji Itadori: Mentor and protector
  • Megumi Fushiguro: Takes special interest in his growth
  • Nobara Kugisaki: Supportive teacher
  • Suguru Geto: Former best friend turned enemy

Themes Around Gojo

  • The burden of being the strongest
  • Isolation due to power
  • Desire to reform the jujutsu system

Impact on the Story

  • Acts as the balance of power in the world
  • Enemies must plan carefully just to deal with him
  • His sealing changes the entire direction of the story
